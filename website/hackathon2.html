Hackathon 2.0: Happy Planet Index Explorer

EPPS 6356 Data Visualization · Assignment 7 · Team Nedret Sen-Cavus, Anugraha Samuel Gloria, Eric Huang Shao-Kang

Our Shiny app links three charts from Abela's Chart Suggestions: A Thought-Starter (2006): Chart 9, a scatter chart; Chart 18, a bubble chart; and Chart 3, a bar chart we chose because the Happy Planet Index gives one score per country across more than 130 countries. Drag a box on the scatter chart to highlight those countries in the bubble chart and rank them in the bar chart; press play on the year slider to animate 2006–2025.

If the app does not load here, open it in a new tab. An idle app can take a little while to start.
